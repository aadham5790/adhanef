Creative Block and How I Break It

Practical strategies for moving through creative resistance without waiting for inspiration.

motivation 2025-08-01 4 min read

Creative block is not a failure of imagination. It is usually a failure of momentum.

When I feel stuck, I do something small and physical: organize files, rename folders, clear the desk. It sounds unrelated, but it changes my state from thinker to mover. Once momentum returns, the ideas usually follow.

The other strategy I use is lowering the stakes. I make something bad on purpose. That removes the pressure of quality and lets me restart from a place of motion instead of hesitation.
