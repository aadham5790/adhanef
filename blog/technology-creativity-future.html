Technology, Creativity and the Future

Exploring how technology and creativity intersect, and what it means for creators building from small places.

tech 2025-09-20 6 min read

Technology and creativity are not opposing forces. They are two ways of solving the same problem: how do we make what we imagine real? In the last few years I have become more interested in the space between them, where tools shape ideas and ideas reshape tools.

Small places matter here. When you do not have a large team or a big budget, you learn to build with constraints. Constraints force decisions. Decisions lead to style. Style becomes voice. That is where creativity starts to feel less like inspiration and more like practice.

The future I am interested in is not a single platform or trend. It is a mindset: curiosity first, utility second, and willingness to iterate in public.
