Building in Public: Experiment Log

A running log of experiments, failures, and small wins from the workshop.

experiment 2025-09-12 4 min read

This is not a success journal. It is a working log of things I tested, things that broke, and the small wins that kept me going.

Last month I tried automating a repetitive content workflow. The first version failed because I optimized too early. The second version failed because I ignored the boring edge cases. The third version worked well enough to keep using.

The pattern is consistent: failure is not the opposite of progress. It is usually part of the path.
