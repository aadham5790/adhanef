Beach Portrait

Natural light portraits by the shore.

photography portrait

Natural light on the beach is beautiful and unforgiving. There is no softbox, no reflector, and no second take once the light changes.

These portraits were made with simple setups: one subject, one direction of light, and a lot of attention to small movements.
