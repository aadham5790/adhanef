Underwater Light

Light studies beneath the surface.

photography landscape

Underwater light behaves differently than above water. It softens, scatters, and creates its own time-of-day mood regardless of what is happening on the surface.

This project is a study of those conditions: shallow water, available light, and minimal equipment.
