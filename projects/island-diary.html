Island Diary

Island Diary is an ongoing personal project documenting the Maldives through a creator's perspective—not just the postcard version, but the small details that make island life unique. Through photography, short stories, and observations, the project captures landscapes, everyday moments, people, and the changing relationship between island life and technology.

photography visual-journal

Island Diary is not a tourism project. It is a record of the version of island life that does not fit into postcards: the slow transitions between day and night, the way technology sits beside tradition, and the small decisions that shape daily life.

The work is ongoing, and it stays personal because that is the only scale at which it still feels honest.
